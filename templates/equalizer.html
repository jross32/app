The Big Picture: Equalizer Scoring

The APA Equalizer system keeps matches competitive by adding a bonus to the games (or points) won in a team match.

  • Team points = games/points won + an Equalizer bonus.
  • A handicap flag (often shown as an “over/under” marker) determines which side gets a smaller or larger bonus.
  • The bonus makes close matches count, so standings reflect both performance and handicapped balance.

8-Ball Matches

8-Ball team scores come from games won across five individual matches, plus a bonus:

  • Games Won: each rack won by the team during the night.
  • Equalizer Bonus: usually a small fixed value; the team giving the handicap takes the smaller bonus.
  • Total Team Points: games won + bonus.

Example: If we win 8 games and take a +3 bonus, our team total is 11 for that match.

9-Ball Matches

9-Ball uses points (balls made) instead of games. The bonus is larger to match the scoring scale.

  • Points Scored: balls made across the night (max 75 per side in standard play).
  • Equalizer Bonus: a fixed value (often 15) adjusted by the handicap flag.
  • Total Team Points: points scored + bonus.

Example: 63 points scored + 15 bonus = 78 total points for the team.
