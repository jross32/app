About This App

Everything for our league season in one dark, mobile-friendly place: dashboards, scouting, AI, and the Coach / Matchup Planner.
Dashboard LAB Analytics Locker Chat Coach Planner AI Answers
What’s Inside
  • Dashboard: hero card, week/next-game pills, division cards, trends, player leaderboard, dues snapshot.
  • Standings & Roster: division tables, highlight for our team, roster by format, player cards.
  • Schedule & Dues: APA-style dates, bye/holiday callouts, results, weekly dues statuses.
  • LAB: Matchup Predictor, Simulation, Lineup Optimizer, Player Trends, Win Probability, Race Calculator, Momentum, Points Projection, Power Rankings, Vegas Path, and more.
  • Locker: channels, DMs, profile pics, badges/patches, message history; new “Cüetillery” gear chat.
  • Profiles: player card, match history modal, patches/badges, comments, theme song, avatar crop, backgrounds/banners, flair.

Theme

Data

How to Use
  1. Start at Dashboard for current week, standings cards, and key trends.
  2. Check Schedule for bye/holiday notes and results; update Dues after each match.
  3. Use LAB tools for matchups, simulations, lineups, race estimates, momentum, and projections.
  4. Chat in Locker channels/DMs; keep profiles updated (player link, avatar, theme, badges/patches).
