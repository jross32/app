# APA Team Manager - APA Rules & Scoring Reference

## 8-Ball Scoring (Division-Specific)
- If a player is **on the hill** (one game away from winning) and wins, they earn **2 points** for their team.
- If a player is on the hill and loses, they earn **1 point** for their team.
- If a player wins and the opponent does **not win a single game**, the winner gets **3 points** for their team, the loser gets **0**.
- The loser always gets **0** unless they are on the hill (then they get 1 point).

## 9-Ball Scoring
- Standard APA 9-ball scoring applies (see team manual for details).

## Bonus Points
- **Bonus points** are awarded for completing dues and paperwork on time:
  - **+3 points** for 8-ball
  - **+15 points** for 9-ball
- These bonuses are already reflected in the final match scores in the data.
- In real matches, if your team gets 7 points in 8-ball, you may actually be awarded 7+3=10 points.

## General APA Team Rules (from Team Manual)
- Team rosters, match schedules, and standings are managed per APA guidelines.
- Dues must be paid weekly and on time to avoid penalties.
- All players must be registered and have valid skill levels.
- Forfeits, substitutions, and match reschedules must follow APA policy.
- Sportsmanship and league etiquette are required at all times.
- For full rules, see the official team manual: `data/team-manual-english.pdf`.

## Using This App
- Use the dashboard to track standings, dues, and schedule.
- Roster and player stats are updated weekly.
- Admins can upload new data files via the Admin Panel (password required).
- All data is validated for APA compliance and integrity.
